Wheeler Dashboard Analytics — Privacy policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Wheeler Dashboard Analytics is a private client reporting dashboard operated by Miguel Casteleiro. This policy explains how the dashboard handles connected reporting accounts and workspace information.

Information the dashboard uses

With the account owner's permission, the dashboard reads reports from the selected Google Analytics and Google Search Console properties. These reports include aggregate users and page views, traffic sources, page and search-query performance, clicks, impressions and configured contact-event counts. The Google permissions are read-only; the dashboard does not change Analytics or Search Console settings.

The dashboard can also read reports from the connected Bing Webmaster Tools account. Workspace users may provide their name, content feedback, approvals and answers to planning questions.

The dashboard does not ask for or store your Google account password.

How information is used

Connected reporting data is used to display and maintain the client's reports and to support the client's marketing measurement. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising targeting, or used to train generalized artificial-intelligence models.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and access

Connection credentials are encrypted before storage in Cloudflare Workers KV. Reporting snapshots and shared workspace records are stored in the private dashboard backend on Cloudflare. The public website interface is hosted using GitHub Pages; private reports and connection credentials are not published in the public site repository.

Workspace access uses a shared password and a signed session. Anyone given that workspace password can access its shared information. Browser storage is used for the session and for preserving pending workspace edits.

The operator and hosting providers process information as necessary to operate and maintain the dashboard. Access is limited to authorized workspace users and maintenance access needed to provide the service.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Connection credentials are kept until the connection is removed or deletion is requested. Reporting snapshots are kept until replaced or deleted. Workspace records are retained to preserve the client's work and review history until their deletion is requested.

You can revoke Google's access at https://myaccount.google.com/connections. Revocation prevents future account access but does not itself delete previously saved dashboard reports. To disconnect a source, request deletion of stored credentials or reports, or ask about workspace information, contact migkast@gmail.com.

Contact and changes

Contact: Miguel Casteleiro, migkast@gmail.com.

Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
