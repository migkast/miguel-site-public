A practitioner's comparison of growth advisory and fractional leadership against a Shopify CRO product and agency service, with pricing, fit, and tradeoffs.

Updated on: 2026-10-09

If you are a founder weighing Miguel Casteleiro against Acceleroi, the first thing to understand is that these are not the same category of thing. Miguel Casteleiro is a growth advisor who either coaches your existing team for three months or steps in as a fractional Head of Growth. Acceleroi is a Shopify conversion-rate optimization product with tiered plans, plus a separate agency arm that runs managed CRO work. One directs your whole growth operation. The other runs a testing loop on your store. The right pick depends almost entirely on what is actually broken.

I keep seeing this confusion in sales calls. A founder says "I'm comparing you to Acceleroi," and after ten minutes it becomes clear they have two different problems wrapped into one question: my store doesn't convert well enough and nobody owns growth across my channels, budgets, and agencies. Those need different answers.

What each one is

Miguel Casteleiro provides hands-on e-commerce growth advisory and fractional leadership for brands doing roughly $2M to $20M in annual revenue. The remit covers six connected areas: unit economics, measurement, acquisition, conversion, retention, and execution. The starting point is usually contribution margin, acquisition boundaries, and payback expectations, then finding the one constraint that is actually holding growth back and assigning owners to fix it. The approach is described as working above routine execution rather than becoming another operator inside your ad accounts.

Acceleroi is a Shopify-focused CRO tool. It runs a free AI audit that ranks conversion leaks, then paid plans build variants and run A/B tests. A test counts when a complete A/B test goes live. The company also sells human-delivered services: a 90-Day CRO Sprint with a six-to-eight person pod and a monthly Growth Retainer. Its pricing page lists in-app plans starting at $299/month and notes the product is in closed beta.

So the honest framing: Miguel is a senior person who takes responsibility for growth direction. Acceleroi is software plus optional agency labor focused on store conversion.

Who does the work, and what that means for you

This is the difference that matters most and the one buyers underweight.

With Miguel's advisory engagement, your current growth leader, team, and agencies stay in place. He diagnoses bottlenecks, sets commercial targets, prioritizes channels and budgets, evaluates whether your agency is actually good, and builds a plan with a review rhythm. The working sessions run weekly or biweekly. You are buying judgment and direction, not more hands in the accounts.

The fractional Head of Growth option is more hands-on. He directs agencies and internal specialists, sets goals and budgets, runs weekly reviews, coaches the team, and owns the plan's accountability. This is for brands that genuinely lack a senior owner for the growth function.

Acceleroi's self-serve plans put the AI in the driver's seat for building and checking variants. On the free first test and Starter, you approve the launch. The Sprint and Managed service add human strategists and a production pod handling design, copy, development, analytics, and email. If what you need is a disciplined testing engine on your Shopify store, that pod is built for exactly that.

Here is the judgment I'd defend: if your dashboards look fine but your margins are thin, if your agencies are each optimizing their own corner, if nobody can tell you your real payback window, a CRO testing loop will not fix that. Those are leadership and measurement problems. Testing more button colors on your product page won't move them.

And the reverse is just as true. If your economics are healthy, your team is aligned, and the single clearest lever is "our store converts at 1.4% and it should be closer to 2.5%," then paying for senior advisory to tell you to test your checkout is overkill. Buy the testing capacity.

Fit by situation

A few patterns that map cleanly to one side:

Pick Miguel Casteleiro when:

  • Growth has stalled and you cannot tell if the problem is your agency, your team, or your strategy
  • ROAS collapses every time you scale budget and nobody knows why
  • You have three agencies and no single commercial plan tying them together
  • Your attribution is a mess and you don't trust your own numbers
  • You need first-party attribution and server-side tracking built properly so decisions rest on reliable data
  • You want to keep your existing team but need senior direction and accountability above them

Pick Acceleroi when:

  • Your economics and channel mix are fine and conversion is the clear constraint
  • You run on Shopify and have at least ~30,000 monthly sessions for meaningful tests
  • You want a repeatable A/B testing program without hiring a CRO team
  • You're comfortable with AI-built variants on lower tiers, or want a strategist-led pod on higher ones

The platform point is worth flagging. Acceleroi's in-app product is Shopify-only for now. If you run on another platform, the self-serve tiers are off the table regardless of fit. Miguel's work is platform-agnostic and spans the full channel set, from Meta and Google to TikTok, Klaviyo, and email.

On the evidence, and reading it honestly

Both sides publish proof you should treat as self-reported.

Miguel's site reports seven years leading a performance marketing agency, work across 130 brands, and $250M in annual revenue at the largest company advised, with named brands including 10DAYS, POLSPOTTEN, and GAUGE81. It also shows selected one-month ad-account snapshots. These are his published experience and platform-reported examples, not audited forecasts or guaranteed outcomes.

Acceleroi's Sprint page reports an average uplift of +31.7% across Sprint clients and individual cases including a 25% revenue increase. Treat those as company-reported results, not independently verified causal estimates. A Clutch listing adds a verified client review, which is useful context but still a review, not a controlled study.

I'd apply the same skepticism to both. Averages across a client base hide variance, and a single month of ad-account improvement rarely tells you whether growth compounded. Ask either party to walk you through a case where things went sideways. The answer tells you more than any uplift stat.

What I would do first

Before you book a call with anyone, spend an afternoon answering one question: is my problem conversion, or is my problem direction?

Pull your contribution margin and payback window. If you cannot calculate them confidently, that is your answer. You have a measurement and economics problem, and no amount of A/B testing fixes a number you can't trust. That points toward advisory or fractional leadership, where setting commercial targets and trusted metrics comes before anything else.

If your economics are solid, your team is aligned, your numbers are clean, and the gap is clearly a store that converts below its potential, then a CRO testing program is the more direct, cheaper fix. Start Acceleroi's free audit, see what it flags, and decide if a testing loop is worth $299 to $2,399 a month to you.

For most brands in the $2M to $20M range that I talk to, the honest diagnosis is "direction first." The conversion gap is real but it sits underneath a bigger problem: no single owner, no commercial plan, and numbers nobody trusts. Fix those and the conversion work gets sharper because you finally know which tests are worth running. That ordering is the whole argument for choosing growth leadership over a testing tool when you can only afford one.

FAQ

Is Acceleroi a direct competitor to Miguel Casteleiro?

Not really. Acceleroi is a Shopify CRO product plus agency services focused on store conversion. Miguel Casteleiro is a growth advisor and fractional leader covering economics, measurement, acquisition, conversion, retention, and team execution. They overlap on conversion and measurement but solve different-sized problems. The useful comparison is between a specific Acceleroi tier and a specific Miguel engagement, not the two brands as equivalents.

Which is cheaper?

Acceleroi's in-app plans start at $299/month, so on raw entry price it's cheaper. But that buys a testing loop, not growth leadership. Miguel's advisory and fractional pricing is scoped after a fit conversation, which makes a clean comparison impossible. Cheaper only matters if the two things solve your actual problem, and usually they don't solve the same one.

Can I use both?

Yes, and for some brands that's the smart play. A fractional Head of Growth or advisory engagement sets the commercial plan and decides where conversion work fits, then a tool like Acceleroi executes the testing program under that direction. Growth leadership handles the "which problem matters" question; a CRO product handles the "run the experiments" part.

Does Acceleroi work if I'm not on Shopify?

The in-app testing product is Shopify-only for now, so its self-serve plans won't apply. Miguel's work is platform-agnostic and spans Meta, Google, TikTok, Klaviyo, email, and CRO regardless of your stack.

How do I know if I need leadership or just testing?

Calculate your contribution margin and payback window. If you can't do it confidently, you have a direction and measurement problem, which points to advisory or fractional leadership. If those numbers are clean and your only clear gap is store conversion, a CRO testing program is the more direct fix.
